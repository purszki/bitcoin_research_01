5) Hierarchical filter PoC structures and matching path
- Added:
  - `src/hierarchical_blockfilters.h`
  - `src/hierarchical_blockfilters.cpp`
- Added `WindowBlockFilter` + `HierarchicalBlockFilters` interface and compile-safe implementation.
- Added hierarchical lookup methods:
  - `Match(const Element&) -> std::optional<size_t>`
